Goals

Our Mission & Vision

A future where food, education, and equal opportunity are never a privilege. Here’s what we’re working toward together.

Our Mission

End hunger as a barrier to dignity

To provide nutritious food to those in need so that hunger never becomes a barrier to a dignified life. Through regular food drives and programs for children and the elderly, we fight malnutrition with compassion.

Our Vision

Equal opportunity for every individual

Build a society where every person—regardless of background—has access to food, education, and equal opportunities to live with dignity.

Save more lives with your help

FAQ

Our Frequently Asked Questions

Explore our FAQs for quick and helpful guidance about our work, donations, and how you can get involved.

Nar Seva Narayan Seva is a non-profit organization dedicated to serving the underprivileged by providing food, education, healthcare, and support for essential needs like marriages. We believe that serving humanity is serving God.
You can donate easily through our online donation portal. We also accept donations via bank transfer, UPI, and other secure payment options. Every contribution goes directly towards helping those in need.
Yes, you can directly sponsor a child’s education or a family’s meal program. We also have special donation packages for school kits, healthcare, and wedding assistance.
We currently operate across multiple regions, focusing on areas with the highest need, based in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. Our goal is to expand our reach to serve more communities.
You can register as a volunteer through the form below or contact us directly. We welcome individuals who wish to give their time, skills, and energy to support our cause.
